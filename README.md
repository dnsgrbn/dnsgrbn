# 👋 Hey, I'm Dennis

It all started innocently: a friend bought a 3D printer. Not long after, there was a Raspberry Pi on my desk running its first Docker containers, installed with commands I half understood.
I didn't carefully dive into this bubble, I cannonballed right in. And no, it has nothing to do with my actual job. It just escalated. 💥

## 🧪 My playground

- 🖨️ **3D printing:** My printer runs more often than my washing machine, and everything I own now has a printed mount.
- 🔧 **DIY:** Why buy it when you can spend three times as long building it?
- 🤖 **Vibe coding:** I bring the idea, the AI does the typing. Afterwards we both pretend that was the plan all along.
- 🖥️ **Servers & homelab:** More containers than my kitchen cupboard, and all of them run at home.
- 🏠 **Smart home:** With Home Assistant I spend two weeks automating things that used to take two seconds.
- 🧰 **Work tools:** little helpers that make the workday less annoying

## 🔭 Right now

- 🔭 **Working on:** a homeserver for my 10" rack, a 3D-printed smart display and my own little dashboard OS
- 🌱 **Learning:** whatever the current project breaks first
- 💬 **Ask me about:** printer settings, Docker containers and why my lights turn on by themselves
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** half of my hardware is held together by things I printed myself

## 📜 House rules

- I build what I need, and sometimes things I only think I need.
- My project backlog grows faster than my storage, and that's already self-hosted.
- "It works" is a fully valid test status. ✅

## 🌊 Offline mode

- 🏄 **Wakeboarding & kitesurfing:** finally somewhere without Wi-Fi…
- 💭 **…planning my next project,** even though three others aren't finished yet.
